# Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

Night Reader (“the app”) is designed to help users read comfortably at night by controlling screen brightness and providing torch-like illumination. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

## Information We Collect
Night Reader **does not collect, store, or share any personal information**.

Specifically:
- No account or login is required
- No personal identifiers are collected
- No location data is collected
- No contacts, photos, or files are accessed
- No usage analytics are collected
- No advertising ID is used

## Permissions Used
The app may request the following permissions **only to function properly**:
- **Display / Brightness access** – to adjust screen brightness for night reading
- **Flashlight access (if applicable)** – to provide torch light functionality

These permissions are used **only on your device** and are never transmitted or stored externally.

## Data Sharing
We do **not** share any data with third parties because **no data is collected**.

## Data Storage
Night Reader does not store personal data locally or on any server.

## Children’s Privacy
Night Reader does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected, the app is safe for general use.

## Changes to This Policy
If this privacy policy changes, updates will be reflected in this document and within the app listing.
